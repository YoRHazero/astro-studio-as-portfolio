A Japanese-learning app for native Chinese speakers, on macOS and Windows. It leaves out the drills that only test reading kanji for their meaning.

An agent builds vocabulary lists and scripts by calling tools, and a locally running Qwen TTS model voices them, so characters you create become your own audio drama. Scripts play in four modes: Japanese only, Chinese only, or one language followed by the other.

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in physics at the University of Tokyo. I study extreme emission line galaxies and the obscured active galactic nuclei that can power them, combining JWST and ground-based observations with Bayesian modelling and photoionization simulations. Before that, I worked on constraining cosmology with fast radio bursts.

I also build the software my research needs, such as a browser-based platform for inspecting JWST slitless spectra, and outside research I make desktop apps of my own. These days I mostly direct coding agents, and spend my own effort on architecture and state-machine design.

I expect to finish my Ph.D. in September 2027.
